# Compliance Screen: Vendor Financing Explainer Video

TOTEM COMPLIANCE SCREEN: Video script (recorded video, member and prospect facing)
Risk Classification: Red
Required Approval: Legal Counsel Review
Final Recommendation: APPROVED WITH EDITS, pending Legal Counsel sign off before public release

The video contains a member economics percentage (the 5% origination fee) and insurance references. Under Section 6 of the manual, any return or fee percentage plus insurance content scores Red and requires Legal Counsel review before the video ships publicly. The build in this folder already applies all rewrites and disclaimers below.

## Section 1: Prohibited language flags (original draft, all fixed in the build)

1. "Fixed 5% return" and "Members earn a fixed five percent on their money". Return language on a percentage is a Red trigger (Category 3, fixed investment return). Rewritten to: "Fixed origination fee, defined per agreement" with the fee framing used on the site.
2. "100% insured, including the 5%". Reads as "fully insured", Category 4 insurance misstatement, and "insured returns" is an auto Red trigger. Rewritten to: "Insurance is structured into the transaction itself" with the full insurance disclaimer displayed adjacent on screen.
3. "Top A-grade global insurance carrier" without the carrier performance qualifier. Pattern 4, implied insurance certainty. Kept as "A-rated global insurance carrier arrangement at the transaction level" with the exact insurance disclaimer on the same screen.
4. Em dashes: 14 instances in the draft. All removed; the finished copy contains none.

## Section 2: Implied meaning flags

1. "It is already in the deal" (narration) implied coverage certainty. The build says "Coverage is arranged inside the transaction from day one" and pairs it with the insurance disclaimer in the same frame.
2. Naming Amazon as the buyer is kept as an illustrative example ("a large institutional buyer, like Amazon"). No logo or trade dress is used, text only. Confirm with counsel that the illustrative use is acceptable for public distribution.

## Section 3: Numbers requiring substantiation files

1. 5% origination fee: substantiate with the standard member agreement showing the contractual fee term.
2. 90 day cycle: substantiate with transaction records showing the defined cycle. The site copy states a 90 to 100 day fixed cycle; if counsel prefers the range, change the stat card to "90 to 100 days".

## Section 4: Required disclaimers (all present in the build)

1. Video open disclaimer, displayed for 6 seconds at the start (manual requires at least 5): "Participation in commercial transactions involves risk, including possible loss of capital. Historical performance does not guarantee future outcomes."
2. Insurance disclaimer, on screen adjacent to the insurance stat cards: "Insurance arrangements, where applicable, are subject to carrier approval, policy terms, exclusions, limitations, claims processes, and carrier performance and do not guarantee transaction performance or repayment."
3. General disclaimer, on the closing frame: "This material is provided for informational purposes only and does not constitute legal, tax, securities, lending, investment, or financial advice. Participation in commercial transactions involves risk, including possible loss of capital. Historical performance does not guarantee future outcomes."

If narration or voiceover is added later, the open disclaimer must also be spoken, not only displayed.

## Section 5: Tone notes

The story keeps its edge: a real squeeze, a real message, a stamped FUNDED moment. The energy lives in the narrative and the visuals rather than in performance promises, which is exactly where the manual wants it.

## Section 6: Final checklist

1. Prohibited terms: PASS after rewrites
2. Em dashes: PASS, zero in finished copy
3. Numbers with substantiation plan: PASS pending the two files above
4. Required disclaimers present and legible: PASS
5. Legal Counsel review before public release: OPEN, required because the video contains a fee percentage and insurance references
